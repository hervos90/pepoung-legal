Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026 · Last updated: October 11, 2026

Pepoung ("we," "us," or "our") provides a personal safety check-in application ("the App," "the Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and authentication credentials (and a phone number, if you choose to provide one).
  • Trusted contact information: the name and email address of any trusted contact you add, and their mobile number if you choose SMS or phone call alerts for that contact. This information is provided by you.
  • Check-in activity: timestamps of check-ins, confirmations, missed check-ins, and escalation events.
  • Location information: collected only at the moment you trigger an SOS alert, in order to share your location with your trusted contacts. We do not continuously track or store your location in the background.
  • Voice message (optional): if you record a personal voice message for your SOS alert, we store the recording so it can be played to your trusted contact during the alert call. You can replace or delete it at any time.
  • Communication logs: records of SMS, voice calls, emails, or push notifications sent as part of the check-in and alert system (event metadata only, not full message content beyond what is needed to operate the Service).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the daily check-in system and detect missed check-ins.
  • To send check-in reminders to you as push notifications in the App.
  • To send missed check-in alerts and SOS notifications to your designated trusted contacts by email, SMS, or phone call, depending on the channels you choose.
  • To obtain the consent of trusted contacts before sending them any text message: we send one invitation text message, and alerts are sent by SMS only after the contact replies YES.
  • To provide customer support and maintain the security of your account.
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information, including mobile phone numbers, with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Phone numbers are used solely to operate the check-in and alert features of the Service.

3. Text Messaging Program, Message Frequency and Carrier Disclosures

Pepoung sends text messages to trusted contacts only. Daily check-in reminders are sent to the account holder as push notifications in the App, not by text message.

When an account holder adds you as a trusted contact and selects SMS alerts for you, we send you one invitation text message. You opt in by replying YES (or OUI), and you can decline by replying NO. After you opt in, you may receive a confirmation message, an alert message if the account holder misses a daily check-in, and an SOS alert message that includes the account holder's location if they trigger an SOS.

Message frequency varies based on the account holder's check-in activity. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for assistance. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of using Pepoung or creating an account, and the same alerts can be received by email.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use trusted third-party providers to operate the Service, including providers for SMS/voice delivery, cloud data storage, subscription billing, and transactional email. These providers process data solely on our behalf and under contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations. They do not use your information, or any mobile opt-in data, for their own purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain check-in and alert event logs for as long as your account is active, and for a limited period afterward as needed for security and support purposes. Voice messages are kept until you replace or delete them, or delete your account. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 7).

6. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers may process or store data outside of your country of residence, including in the United States and Canada. Where required by applicable law, including Québec law for residents of Québec, we take reasonable steps to ensure an adequate level of protection for any personal information transferred in this way.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to receive a copy of it in a structured format, or to withdraw consent to receive messages. To exercise these rights, contact us at herveguifodev@gmail.com. Trusted contacts may also opt out of receiving any further text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message they receive. Residents of Québec may also file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 14 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction) without the consent and involvement of a parent or guardian acting as the account holder.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes through the App or by email.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy, and requests to the person in charge of the protection of personal information, can be sent to herveguifodev@gmail.com.
