Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026 · Last updated: September 12, 2026

Pepoung ("we," "us," or "our") provides a personal safety check-in application ("the App," "the Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and authentication credentials.
  • Trusted contact information: the name and phone number (and optionally email) of any trusted contact you add, provided by you.
  • Check-in activity: timestamps of check-ins, confirmations, missed check-ins, and escalation events.
  • Location information: collected only at the moment you trigger an SOS alert, in order to share your location with your trusted contacts. We do not continuously track or store your location in the background.
  • Communication logs: records of SMS, voice calls, or push notifications sent as part of the check-in and alert system (event metadata only, not full message content beyond what is needed to operate the Service).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the daily check-in system and detect missed check-ins.
  • To send check-in reminders, missed check-in alerts, and SOS notifications to your designated trusted contacts.
  • To verify consent from trusted contacts before sending them any messages.
  • To provide customer support and maintain the security of your account.
We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or your trusted contacts' phone numbers with third parties for marketing purposes. Phone numbers are used solely to operate the check-in and alert features of the Service.

3. Message Frequency and Carrier Disclosures

By opting in, you may receive: one daily check-in message, occasional reminder messages if a check-in is missed, and alert messages sent to your trusted contacts when applicable. Message frequency varies based on your account settings and check-in activity. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for assistance.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use trusted third-party providers to operate the Service, including providers for SMS/voice delivery, cloud data storage, subscription billing, and transactional email. These providers process data solely on our behalf and under contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations — they do not use your information for their own purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain check-in and alert event logs for as long as your account is active, and for a limited period afterward as needed for security and support purposes. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 7).

6. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers may process or store data outside of your country of residence, including in the United States and Canada. Where required by applicable law, we take reasonable steps to ensure an adequate level of protection for any personal information transferred in this way.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to withdraw consent to receive messages. To exercise these rights, contact us at herveguifodev@gmail.com. Trusted contacts may also opt out of receiving any further messages at any time by replying STOP to any message they receive.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction) without the consent and involvement of a parent or guardian acting as the account holder.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes through the App or by email.

10. Contact Us
